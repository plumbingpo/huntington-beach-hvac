Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Francisco

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Take a look at the age of your system. That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

Minor issues rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
